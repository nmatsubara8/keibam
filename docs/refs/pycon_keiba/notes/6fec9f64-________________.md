For learning machine learning, the speaker suggests a structured approach to maximize understanding:

1.  **Foundation:** Start by studying a single, systematically written textbook.
2.  **Application:** After mastering the fundamentals, attempt to apply the knowledge by working on various projects.

This sequential approach is suggested as a 'shortcut' to gaining comprehensive understanding.
